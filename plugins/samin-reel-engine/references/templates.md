# Reel templates grounded in the source corpus

These are beat scaffolds, not fill-every-slot formulas. Each numbered item is one spoken paragraph. The shortform target is normally 140–175 words and around 9 beats; observed standard reels span 98–195 words and 7–12 beats. Combine or remove beats when a story needs less. Source labels V1–V30 refer to the September Reels corpus.

## New tool or capability

Anchors: V1, V3, V4, V10, V15, V21, V24, V26.

1. You can [specific outcome] with [specific thing]. / This [file/tool] lets Claude [concrete capability].
2. It’s called [name], and it [plain-language mechanism].
3. [Install/connect/upload] [input].
4. So when you [concrete task], it [specific behavior].
5. [One useful example with visible input and output].
6. You can also [second capability, only if verified and relevant].
7. [Optional catch, limit, setup requirement, or better use case].
8. So [practical payoff linked to the initial problem].
9. Comment [KEYWORD] and I’ll send you the [existing setup/file/list].

V1 reaches its point in seven beats. V10 expands four rules into their own lines. A tool need not have a made-up metric to fit this form.

## Build this workflow

Anchors: V2, V11, V12, V14, V22, V27, V28.

1. You can turn [tool] into [specific useful system].
2. [What the system actually does, or one curiosity escalator].
3. First, [input or connection that makes the workflow possible].
4. Then [second action and the reason it matters].
5. [Third action; explain where the output goes].
6. [Concrete scenario showing the assembled workflow].
7. [Feedback/approval step or another real dependency].
8. So you [clear result from the pieces assembled].
9. Comment [KEYWORD] and I’ll send you the [workflow/setup/prompts].

The V2 variant introduces role one → opposing role → investor → judge. The V22 variant turns a single visual scenario into the middle of the reel. Keep the workflow plausible end to end; a collection of APIs is not proof the integration works.

## Small curated stack

Anchors: V6, V7, V8, V18, V25.

1. These [real count] [tools/plugins/prompts] help you [one shared outcome].
2. [Optional reason to care].
3. First is [name]. It [one distinct job].
4. Then there’s [name], which [second distinct job].
5. [Name] [third distinct job].
6. [Add fourth/fifth only if the word budget supports it].
7. [How the pieces complement each other, or a real shared access detail].
8. Comment [KEYWORD] and I’ll send you [all N / the full list].

Do not claim five things and deliver four. Keep prices and account requirements per item in the giveaway. A list is stronger when the items do different jobs in a useful workflow.

## Method / fix a recurring mistake

Anchors: V9, V16, V29.

1. [Concrete mistake or missing ingredient] is why [recognizable problem].
2. [Brief invitation to fix it, if needed].
3. First, [change the inputs or define the task].
4. [What that enables, shown through an example].
5. Then [tools/environment/reference structure].
6. [Explain the result of that change].
7. [Iteration or verification step].
8. So [why the method works, expressed as a practical change].
9. Comment [KEYWORD] and I’ll send you the [checklist/template/system].

Attribute a named person's method only when its source supports the exact steps. Do not attribute a newly assembled workflow to Karpathy or another person to borrow authority.

## Access changed / free tier

Anchors: V13, plus V8's free stack and V1's concise capability explanation.

1. [Company] [specific dated change to access].
2. You can [precisely scoped capability] on [tier or eligibility].
3. [Where to get access].
4. [How a viewer connects or uses it].
5. [Specific experiment or project enabled].
6. [Material quota, trial period, eligibility or production-use boundary].
7. So [benefit without implying unlimited free service].
8. Comment [KEYWORD] and I’ll send you the [access/setup guide].

“Open source,” “source available,” “free tier” and “free API” are different claims. Preserve the source's actual access terms. Use an evergreen hook when the change has no verified recent date.

## Case study with a reproducible mechanism

Anchor: V30; related step-by-step format V20 and V22.

1. [Named person or attributed source] got [verified result] with [specific workflow].
2. And it started with [surprisingly ordinary input].
3. First, they [capture/create that input].
4. Then [second asset or preparation step].
5. They gave [specific inputs] to [tool] and asked for [specific transformation].
6. [Generation/assembly step].
7. [Iteration or fix that makes the workflow credible].
8. So [recap of the unexpectedly small set of ingredients].
9. Comment [KEYWORD] and I’ll send you the [recreated workflow].

Separate views from followers, revenue from profit, and reported results from reproduced results. If the number cannot be verified, write an outcome-led workflow reel without the number or select a different story.

## Service / money idea

Anchors: V5, V17, V20, V23. Their historical income claims are not independently validated by this corpus.

1. [Named tools] can turn [input] into [specific service deliverable].
2. [Who would need it and why].
3. Find [concrete customer problem].
4. [Input → transformation step].
5. [Build the finished customer-facing deliverable].
6. [Quality check or recurring update step].
7. Package [exact components] as [service].
8. [Evidence-backed pricing/result, or explicitly framed illustrative arithmetic outside the spoken script].
9. Comment [KEYWORD] and I’ll send you the [workflow/offer template].

Default to a service concept without income promises. A real case-study source can justify a reported result. Do not turn a hypothetical price × customer count into earned monthly revenue.

## Short reaction promo (separate format)

Anchor: V19 only. Keep reference clip and spoken lines separate. The clean spoken body is four beats, roughly 35 words by this project's tokenizer: opinion hook → free offer → short self-aware aside → comment CTA. One sample is not enough to generalize this as the default reel style. Use only when requested or when a real reaction clip and an existing offer are available.

## Story brief before drafting

```yaml
id: story-001
archetype: new-tool
angle: A concrete outcome for a named viewer
source_urls: []
event_date: null
checked_at: YYYY-MM-DD
freshness: recent-event | evergreen | unverified
verified_claims: []
reported_claims: []
unknowns: []
mechanism_beats: []
visual_proof: []
hook_options: []
selected_hook: ""
keyword: ONEWORD
giveaway_title: ""
giveaway_path: ""
reference_samples: [V1, V3, V21]
target_words: 160
```

Keep hook alternatives in the brief, choose one before producing the clean script. Story links, claim evidence, graphics and giveaway instructions belong alongside the reel, not in the spoken text.
