# Editorial judgment

For each spoken beat, write the viewer question, the visual job, the exact claim, the source, the visible detail and the expected takeaway. Reject images that merely share a noun with the script.

## Evidence and placement

Prefer a real relevant action or source excerpt. Establish source identity, then crop to the useful detail without removing qualifications. Label authored reconstructions when they could be mistaken for a live result. An attractive mockup is not execution proof.

Anchor changes to the important spoken word. Keep subject, payoff and relationship readable in the first frame. In the opening, prefer unchanged image holds of0.5s or less and never exceed1s without a user-directed exception. Use real motion and purposeful view changes; body reading holds may need longer.

## Review

Inspect the actual encoded output at phone size and at1× with sound. Record which words must be read, when they become fully visible, caption/focal collisions, voice continuity, music audibility and SFX masking. Keep signal measurements separate from listening and still checks separate from motion review. Repair failures and verify the current render hash.

See the packaged shot recipes, visual-evidence rules and quality procedure for the detailed rubric. Historical private audit files are not part of this public package; create fresh records for your own reel.
